## 1. Architectural Philosophy & Circulation
The villa is engineered with monumental yet proportional zoning separating public reception, intimate family living, culinary entertainment, executive work, private wellness, and deluxe suites.
Circulation is organized around a grand reception gallery and an upper sky bridge with uninterrupted visual lines extending toward the pool terrace and landscaped gardens.

## 2. Floor-by-Floor Architectural Zoning

### Ground Floor (Elevation: 0.00m, Clear Ceiling Height: 3.00m, Cut Height: 1.50m)
1. **01 — Grand Entrance Foyer (`room_gf_foyer`):** Monumental 24 m² marble vestibule featuring a double pivot front entrance door (1.6m x 2.4m) and seamless transitions to reception salon and formal dining hall.

6. **06 — Private Pantry & Service Room (`room_gf_pantry`):** 12 m² service suite housing pet grooming amenities and automated garment care cabinets.
7. **07 — Executive Guest Bedroom (`room_gf_guest_bed`):** 48 m² luxury guest retreat featuring French oak parquet and armored safe integration.
8. **08 — Private Guest Bathroom (`room_gf_guest_bath`):** 24 m² en-suite marble bathroom with couture dressing vanity and luxury sanitaryware.
