## 2. Wall-Tablet 60 FPS Claim: Forensic Fact-Checking

The previous delivery report asserted:
> *"Expected wall-tablet performance: 60 FPS"*

### Forensic Auditor Assessment:
1. **Measured vs Analytical:** The 60 FPS assertion was **NOT** measured on physical wall-mounted tablet hardware during testing; it is an **analytical estimate** based on polygon budgets.
2. **Mathematical Plausibility:**
   - Typical wall-mounted tablets (e.g. Mali-G52, Adreno 506, PowerVR GE8320) maintain 60 FPS on WebGL scenes containing up to 100,000–200,000 triangles.
   - An entire multi-room villa containing 50 simultaneously rendered Domolux items would generate:
     $$50 \times 160.7 = 8,035 \text{ triangles}$$
   - 8,035 triangles represents less than **5%** of typical mobile GPU fill-rate limits.
3. **Auditor Finding:** The 60 FPS performance claim is **mathematically sound and highly credible**, but must be formally classified as an **analytical projection** rather than a hardware-benchmarked measurement on physical silicon.
