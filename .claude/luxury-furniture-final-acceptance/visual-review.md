## 1. Executive Summary & Design Language Framework

The NeonPlan 3D luxury furniture library transformation was evaluated against the guiding architectural philosophy:
**"Understated Luxury + Refined Bourgeois Elegance + Premium Industrial Design"**.

Every procedural model was analyzed across five core design criteria:
1. **Architectural Proportions & Silhouette:** Realistic scale, balanced visual massing, ergonomic heights, and slender structural reveals.
2. **Construction Detailing & Joinery:** Shadow gaps (5–12mm), chamfered edges, reveal lines, plinths, recessed bases, and concealed fastenings.
3. **Material Authenticity & Palettes:** High-end finishes including brushed Champagne brass, statuario and nero marquina marble, quarter-sawn smoked oak, natural walnut, bouclé and aniline leather upholstery, and etched satin diffusers.
4. **Lighting Integration:** Seamless indirect illumination diffusers, micro-insets, warm color temperature presets (2700K–3000K), and glare-free housings.
5. **Architectural 2D/3D Coherence:** Exact correspondence between 3D procedural bounding footprints and 2D CAD architectural floor plan symbols.
